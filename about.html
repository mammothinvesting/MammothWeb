About Mammoth Investing Inc

We're a team of innovators dedicated to democratize financial literacy and build wealth for all.

Our Mission

At Mammoth, our mission is to empower everyone to make smarter, more informed investment decisions through cutting-edge AI technology. We believe financial success should be accessible to all, not just the experts.

Our goal is to deliver intuitive, data-driven actionable insights that help you confidently navigate the stock market and make financially informed decisions.

Our Values

  • Empowerment: We equip every investor with the tools and insights to confidently shape their financial future.
  • Innovation: Our cutting-edge AI technology drives smarter, data-driven investment decisions.
  • Accessibility: We make navigating the stock market simple and inclusive for all.
  • Education: We champion financial literacy, helping users learn and grow with every investment choice.

Founder Story

I'm Sahil Singh, Founder of MammthAI Investing Inc. For 10+ years, I worked in Data & Analytics whilst learning how investing really works! In the beginning of my investing journey like many new investors, I poured my hard-earned savings into the market, only to face a steep learning curve filled with confusion, overwhelming information, and a lack of clear, actionable guidance.

Determined to turn this vision into reality, I teamed up with my cousin, who joined as co-founder, and together we embarked on the rollercoaster of building a fintech startup. Through every hurdle, our shared passion for democratizing investing kept us going.

A pivotal moment in our journey came with the guidance of Mr. Michael Derchin, whose investing wisdom profoundly shaped our app's approach. Today, MammthAI Investing stands as a testament to our mission: to improve financial literacy & build generational wealth.
